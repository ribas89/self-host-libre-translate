Self Host Libre Translate

Privacy and server choices

This Firefox extension translates text using a LibreTranslate server you choose. No server address is bundled or selected automatically.

What is transmitted?

When you enable page translation, the extension sends readable webpage text to your chosen server. The selection command sends text you select. Automatic language detection may send page-text samples. Translation can continue as you navigate in an enabled tab.

Your server, your choice

You are responsible for choosing, administering and trusting your translation server. HTTP and HTTPS addresses are supported. HTTP traffic may be readable or modified by others on the network. The extension does not operate or audit your chosen server, and server operators may have their own data-handling practices.
